# Template: batches, sample, and the calendar question

**Batch limit:** never more than 20 organizations in one batch.

## Very first run: sample of 5
> **Let's start with a sample of 5 organizations.**
>
> **Why:** the first time, it helps you see exactly what I do and check that it looks right before I do more.

## After finding sources (before touching the calendar)
Show a short table (organization, what I found) and the events found. Then ask explicitly:

> **I found these events:** <short list: name, date, place, organization>
>
> **Should I add them to your calendar?**
> Say "yes", "no", or tell me which to leave out.

Only add after a yes. This question is required every batch, unless the person has said earlier to add automatically (see "Do all" below).

## After adding events: report, then what next
> **See the results here:** [<project name> sources](<sheet link>)
>
> **Calendar:** I added <A> events and updated <U>.
>
> **What next?**
> I can do **10 more**, do **all remaining** (<N> left, in rounds of 20), or stop here. (Suggested: 10 more.)

## Do all
Do consecutive batches of 20. Before starting, say roughly how long it may take and ask once: "Add events to the calendar automatically as I go, or ask you after each round of 20?" Pause after each round with the sheet link and a one-line summary, and let them stop any time.
