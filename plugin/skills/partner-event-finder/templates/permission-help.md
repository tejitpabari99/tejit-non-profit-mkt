# Template: help turning on permissions (use when a connection is missing)

For each missing connection, send:

> I can't reach **<connection name>** yet. That's normal on a first run.
>
> **What it is:** <one plain sentence, e.g. "Google Drive is where your files live.">
> **Why I need it:** <e.g. "to create a folder for this project and save the results there.">
> **Is it safe?** You stay in control. You sign in with your own Google account, you can switch it off any time, and I will tell you exactly what I'll use it for before I use it.
>
> **How to turn it on:**
> 1. In your AI assistant, open its **Settings** and look for **Connectors**, **Integrations** or **Apps** (the name differs from tool to tool).
> 2. Find **<connection name>** and click **Connect**. Sign in with the Google account your team uses.
> 3. Back in this chat, make sure **<connection name>** is switched on for this conversation (often in a menu or **+** button near the message box).
>
> Tell me when you've done it, or say "I'm stuck" and tell me what you see on your screen. I'll help.

After they reply, check again. Repeat until every needed connection works. Needed: Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, web search and page reading.

**Optional connections never block.** If **GitHub** (or any connection marked optional) is not connected, carry on. Do not walk the person through connecting it unless they ask. If they do ask, use the same message above, and tell them it is only for starring the project and sending feedback, and that it is optional.
