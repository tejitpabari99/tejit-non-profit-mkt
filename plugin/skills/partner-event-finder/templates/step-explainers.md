# Template: explainers (before and after each step)

Say it in chat first, then ask the question. Always bold labels, each on its own line, blank line between.

**Before** a step:

> **Next:** <step name>
>
> **Why:** <purpose in one or two plain sentences>
>
> **What I'll do:** <actions>

**After** a step:

> **Done:** <what happened, with simple numbers>
>
> **Next up:** <next step>

## Per-step wording
- **Project folder:** Next: setting up your project folder. / Why: everything this tool makes stays in one tidy place you can find easily, apart from your own files. / What I'll do: create it in Google Drive (you can share or delete it any time).
- **Partner list:** Next: finding your partner list. / Why: that's the list I will look up events for. / What I'll do: find it and read it. I never change it.
- **Data check:** Next: checking what I'll use from your list. / Why: so I read the right information. / What I'll do: read your list and show you what I plan to use: organization name, website, category and city (when you have them). Nothing is created yet.
- **Categories:** Next: choosing categories. / Why: lists often include partners that don't hold events (for example funders). Keeping only relevant ones saves time. / What I'll do: show the categories and ask which to keep. Nothing is removed from your original.
- **New sheet:** Next: creating your new sheet. / Why: it holds the organizations you chose and the event links I find, apart from your original list. / What I'll do: create it in your project folder and copy over only the organizations you kept.
- **Calendar:** Next: the events calendar. / Why: events go in their own calendar so you can share it with your board and keep your own uncluttered. / What I'll do: show you how to make one (about a minute).
- **Saving your choices:** Next: remembering your choices. / Why: so next time you don't have to answer these questions again. / What I'll do: save a small note in your project folder. You never need to open it.
- **Finding sources:** Next: finding where each partner posts events. / Why: events are often one click away from the home page. / What I'll do: visit their websites and note down the event links, plus a short description, website and category in your sheet.
- **Adding to calendar:** Next: adding the events I found. / Why: this is the payoff, one place to see what's coming. / What I'll do: show you the events and add them only after your yes.
