# Template: batches, sample, and the calendar report

**Batch limit:** never more than 20 organizations in one batch.

## Very first run: sample of 5
> **Let's start with a sample of 5 organizations.**
>
> **Why:** the first time, it helps you see exactly what I do and check that it looks right before I do more.

## After finding sources
Show a short table (organization, what I found). Do not ask whether to add the events: add them directly to the project calendar, then report (next section). Unsure matches become a question and write nothing.

## After adding events: report (step 5), then what next (step 6)
Show this once, after any collected questions, not when the events are added.

> **See the results here:** [<project name> sources](<sheet link>)
>
> **Calendar:** I added <A> events and updated <U>.
>
> **Added:** <short list: name, date, place, organization>
>
> **What next?**
> I can do **10 more**, do **all remaining** (<N> left, in rounds of 20), or stop here. (Suggested: 10 more.)

## Do all
Do consecutive batches of 20. Before starting, say roughly how long it may take. Events are added to the project calendar as each round finishes. Pause after each round with the sheet link and a one-line summary, and let them stop any time.
