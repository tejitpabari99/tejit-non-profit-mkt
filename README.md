# Tejit's Non Profit Tools

An open-source plugin for AI assistants. It adds small, guided helpers that take repetitive work off a nonprofit team's plate. Created by Tejit Pabari.

## Who it is for
Nonprofit staff and volunteers who are not technical. Every step is explained in plain words, and nothing happens without your yes.

## Skills
- **`start`**: Introduces the toolkit and helps you pick a skill.
- **`partner-event-finder`**: Finds partner fundraising events and adds them to a shared Google Calendar.
